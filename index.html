Why We Are Different

Completely Automated Trading Technology (ATT)

Alpha-Flow can trade at lightning speed. Sometimes entering and exiting hundreds of microtrades a day. This allows it to capitalize on small and rapid movements that are beyond the scope or ability of any human trader. Everything is fully automated with absolutely no interaction required. This ‘hands off done-for-you’ solution means it is suitable for anyone, regardless of experience.

Beyond AI

Our algorithm is not simply trained to spot patterns. Instead it uses highly advanced machine learning to continually evolve with the market. Each trade is analysed in order to gain better insight as it builds its vast knowledge base. This level of self learning puts Alpha-Flow in a completely separate category to most other trading systems as evidenced by its industry leading performance.

Risk Management

Nearly all trading algorithms are focused primarily on execution. Meaning they rely on a set of parameters and technical analysis to determine when to enter or exit trades. Alpha-Flow’s primary focus is not execution. It is risk management. Aligned parameters trigger an initial query. This is then assessed through our proprietary system of advanced risk analysis. It also factors market sentiment. This is done by reading and monitoring dozens of financial newsfeeds in real time. Giving context, emotional weight and rationale to its decision making process. All in the blink of an eye. Factoring risk before execution is what gives Alpha-Flow such a high win rate.

We Don't Want Your Money

Many trading solutions charge large up front fees sometimes running into thousands of dollars. Meaning if their system doesn’t work, they’ve still been paid. We feel that’s wrong. With Alpha-Flow Sentinel there’s no setup, management or ongoing fees (applies to current invitations only). Instead we work on a performance based system. In other words, we don’t want your money. We simply want your money to win.

You Have Control

Your funds never come to us. They stay in your account with your broker. You are in full control at all times. You can pause, restart, top up your account, withdraw or close your account at any time. There is no minimum period and you only pay a percentage of the profits you make, after you have made them. Meaning we are the ones assuming the risk of not being paid. Not you. Everything we do is transparent and fair and all past results are independently verified.

What makes Alpha-Flow different from other trading software or hedge fund management?

Most trading bots are focused on trade execution using popular strategies or pre-set rules. In other words, if certain conditions, indicators or parameters are met then it triggers an action (buy or sell). This relies heavily on predicting the future based on the past and assumes the market will behave the same way. The reality is markets are unpredictable, and this is why most trading bots only work for a limited time. Alpha-Flow is different. Very different. Using cutting edge machine learning it prioritises risk management over execution. It also uses advanced AI to read, understand an interpret market sentiment. Something no ‘strategy’ can do. In addition, it is self-learning and teaches itself whenever it wins or loses. Currently there is no other AI that we know of that can combine risk, execution and sentiment with machine learning at the speed and level Alpha-Flow has
